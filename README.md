### About Me

Double majoring in **UX Design** and **Interactive Development**, which suits me perfectly since I've never liked choosing between the pretty part and the working part. My strengths live right at the seam between **frontend coding** and **design**, so that's usually where you'll find me.

I get genuinely giddy about the creative side of coding. Group work brings out my favorite kind of problem solving, since good ideas tend to get better once a few more heads are on them. What I care about most is the journey a project takes, from messy first sketch to tidy final version, not just the finished product sitting at the end of it.

Also very fond of game design and interactive storytelling, in case that wasn't already obvious.

### Featured Project

**The Crossroads Exchange**
A self-initiated, browser-based trading game built with PHP and MySQL. It leans on archetype-based gameplay and morally ambiguous trader encounters with no explicit "good" or "bad" labels attached, so the player's own choices do all the talking.
